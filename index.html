How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages and features you need. After a short call you get a fixed quote in PKR, so the price never changes halfway through.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. Your plan includes a firm launch date before any work starts, and you see progress along the way.

Do you only work with businesses in Islamabad?

No. The studio is in G-11/2, Islamabad, and clients include businesses in Rawalpindi and Karachi. Everything can run over WhatsApp and video calls.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, if you want to. Simple editing for things like prices, photos and offers can be set up, and you get a walkthrough on how to use it.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. We can register your domain and set up hosting, or work with what you already have.

What are Tapify cards?

NFC cards and stands from our sister brand Tapify. Customers tap them with their phone to open your Google review page. See tapvate.com.
